# Product requirements

The authoritative complete requirements are preserved verbatim in MASTER_DIRECTIVE.md.

ORBIA: Make room for what matters. Turkish: Önemli olana yer aç.

Android-first Flutter stable/Dart; offline-first Drift/SQLite; Riverpod state; English and Turkish ARB localization. No accounts, analytics, advertisements or hidden telemetry. App ID com.orbia.productivity is provisional. Trademark and store-name clearance required before publication.

Primary destinations: Today, Tasks, Goals, Habits, More. More exposes Calendar, Notes, Focus Timer, Insights, Settings, Data Management, About. Include onboarding, global search, editors and details. All visible actions must work and persist. No fabricated demo metrics.

Delivery requires domain/database/widget/integration tests, responsive and accessibility checks, successful debug APK build, and physical-device QA after explicit authorization. Passing compilation alone is insufficient.

No implementation or acceptance is claimed at this checkpoint.
